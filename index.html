01 / Funding

Find a path to the capital your idea needs.

Funding can come from many places. Start by understanding what you need and which opportunities fit your stage.

  • Estimate startup costs before applying.
  • Check eligibility and deadlines directly.
  • Be cautious of guaranteed-funding promises and upfront fees.

02 / Connections

You don't have to build alone.

The right conversation can save time, reveal a resource, or help you avoid an expensive mistake.

  • Ask specific questions.
  • Explain the problem your business is solving.
  • Follow up and thank people for their time.
